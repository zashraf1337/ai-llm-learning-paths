The honest take — how the career paths fit you

All paths sit on the same ML base, so do Foundations first. After that they differ a lot in how directly your skills transfer and how fast you reach real, paid work. Ranked for your starting point:

Best fit · #1

Red-teaming / LLM security

Your security instincts transfer almost 1:1. Shortest ramp to paid work, booming demand, real bugs in week one. Your differentiated edge.

Fit: very high · ramp: fast
Best fit · #2

AI Engineer (agentic)

The fastest general SWE→AI on-ramp. Agentic apps (RAG, agents, MCP) are the hottest hiring area and need engineering, not a PhD. Pairs perfectly with security.

Fit: very high · ramp: fast
Strong fit · #3

Building & fine-tuning

Hot and doable solo. Applied fine-tuning / post-training leans on engineering. Reach for it when off-the-shelf models aren't enough.

Fit: high · ramp: medium
Strong fit · #4

Infra & MLOps

Your SWE/systems muscles directly. MLOps/LLMOps hires fast; the GPU/kernel niche is a durable, hard-to-fill edge.

Fit: high · ramp: medium
Longest road · #5

AI researcher

Math- and publication-gated (often a PhD). The realistic on-ramp is research engineer (ARENA, interpretability, reproductions). Pursue for depth, not a near-term job.

Fit: medium · ramp: slow

Your unfair advantage: combine #1 and #2. Security + AI/agentic engineering = AI/agentic security engineer — the person who can build agentic systems and break them. That niche is scarce, premium, and exactly where your background points.

Suggested sequence (part-time)

Phase 0 · ~weeks 1–8

Foundations

fast.ai + Karpathy "Zero to Hero", math intuition just-in-time. Outcome: you can train and actually understand models.

Phase 1 · ~weeks 6–18

Spearhead: AI Engineer + Security

Build agentic apps (RAG → agents → MCP → evals) and red-team them (HackAPrompt → OWASP/ATLAS → tooling). The two reinforce: you attack what you build.

Phase 2 · ~weeks 14–24

Build & fine-tune

When prompting/RAG isn't enough: HF LLM course → Unsloth fine-tune → post-training → Raschka's book. Adds custom-model depth to your engineering.

Phase 3 · weeks 22+

Infra/MLOps depth & edge

Pick by traction: LLMOps/serving, GPU/kernels, distributed training, or advanced security/interpretability. Researcher track = slow burn alongside.

How to read the ratings. "Fit" is calibrated to you (strong engineer, newer to ML, security-leaning), not the general public — a course can be world-class and still "Situational" for your goals. "The honest take" is where I flag caveats (dated material, prerequisites, hype vs. substance).

Cost note. Most "Paid" picks are Coursera / DeepLearning.AI (≈$49/mo, cancel anytime), one-off books (~$30–60), or cheap Udemy courses (often ~$15–25 on sale). The one expensive item is the AI Red Teaming Masterclass (AIRTP+, ~$1,199), flagged where it appears.
